World In A Cell is a VR educational game by USC World Building Media Lab that helps students understand complex cell biology.

As VR UX Designer, I led interaction flow and UI design, and organized 30+ usability and scenario tests.

Project outcomes:

- Improved immersion and learning efficiency;
- Used testing to solve operation and comprehension pain points;
- Collaborated with biological scientists on a classroom VR tool;
- Introduced into USC biology courses in Fall 2023.
