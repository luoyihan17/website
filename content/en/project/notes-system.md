World in a Cell is a VR educational game by USC World Building Media Lab that helps students understand complex cell biology.

As VR UX Designer, I led the interaction flow and UI design, and organized 30+ usability and scenario-based tests.

Project outcomes:

- Improved immersion and learning efficiency;
- Used testing to identify and solve interaction and comprehension issues;
- Collaborated with biology researchers on a classroom VR tool;
- Helped introduce the project into USC biology courses in Fall 2023.
