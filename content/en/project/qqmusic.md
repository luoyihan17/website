AI Creation was the core AI music project I worked on at QQ Music. It allowed users to create a full song from a sentence, a conversation, an image, a hummed melody, or a selected style.

The product first tested whether QQ Music users wanted to make music with AI. It then connected AI songwriting, AI singing, image-to-song, remixing, AIMV, publishing, rankings, creator recognition, and business incentives into works that could be played, shared, promoted, and monetized.

## Why Would Users Want to Make a Song?

When I worked on AI songwriting, I did not start from what AI could generate. I started from whether everyday QQ Music users had a real reason to make a song. They needed clear scenarios, usable material, and a next step after generation; otherwise, the feature would remain a novelty.

So my key design question was:

> How can we make the first step easier, then create reasons to pay and share?

</section>

I focused on four challenges:

- **Scenario-based entry points**: Bring fan support, holidays, relationships, and trending topics to the front so users know why to create.
- **Playful remixes**: Turn old-song memes and trending topics into one-tap adaptations.
- **Rankings and recognition**: Use plays, charts, and certificates to make AI songs visible.
- **Business loop**: Connect generation to paid release, promotion, rewards, and retention.

### 1. Scenario-based Creation Entry
![QQ Music AI songwriting user flow](/assets/images/AIMusic/userflow.webp)

I first mapped the **AI songwriting user flow**: choose a scenario, enter content, generate a song, then publish or share it.

For example, QQ Music has a large fan community, so I placed **fan support and confession** scenarios and other **high-motivation use cases** upfront. After choosing an artist, users could turn what they wanted to say into a lightweight support song. My work was to break AI songwriting into clear steps: **scenario entry, material guidance, simple creation, and shareable output**.


### 2. Trending Adaptation and Playful Creation

![QQ Music AI song adaptation](/assets/images/AIMusic/Song%20Adaptation.webp)

AI song adaptation was a key feature in the **playful creation** scenario. Inspired by nostalgic songs being remixed into meme versions on Douyin, I designed a **one-tap adaptation flow**: **choose a topic, generate lyrics, then make simple choices around voice and style**. Users could get something topical, emotional, and shareable without touching complex parameters.

### 3. Ranking and Honor Incentives

![QQ Music AI creation ranking and rewards](/assets/images/AIMusic/Ranking%20List%20%26%20Rewards.webp)

To encourage repeat creation, I proposed **rankings, chart support, and creator certificates**. This moved AI songs from one-off files into a public creative space where works could be seen, compared, and displayed. Through a **post-creation feedback loop**, users could see performance, build a creator identity, and feel encouraged to create or share again.

### 4. Commercial Incentive Loop

![QQ Music AI creation business conversion](/assets/images/AIMusic/Business%20Conversion.webp)

For commercialization, I guided users from "trying it once" toward higher-value **creation and creator services**. **Paid release, music promotion, and reward mechanisms** helped AI songs support conversion, creator retention, and content growth. My work was to make these business paths appear naturally when users wanted better results, more exposure, or more reward.

## Project Summary

This project clarified a simple point for me: AI songwriting is not about placing generation capability in front of users. The key is giving them a concrete reason to create. QQ Music users may not begin with the goal of "making a song," but they do have real motivations such as **fan support, trending expression, relationship communication, and playful participation**.

My design turned those motivations into a lightweight path: **scenario-based entry points** answer "why create," **material guidance and simple creation** lower the barrier, **rankings, chart support, and creator certificates** provide feedback, and **paid release, music promotion, and reward mechanisms** connect to higher-value needs. The value was turning one-off AI generation into a playable, shareable music creation ecosystem.
