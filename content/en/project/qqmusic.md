AI Creation was the core AI music project I worked on at QQ Music. Users could create a full song through a sentence, conversation, image, humming, or style choice.

The product first tested whether QQ Music users wanted to make music with AI. It then connected AI songwriting, AI singing, image-to-song, remixing, AIMV, publishing, rankings, honors, and business incentives into works that could be played, shared, and operated.

## Why Would Users Want to Make a Song?

When I worked on AI songwriting, I did not start from what AI could generate. I started from whether everyday QQ Music users had a real reason to make a song. They need clear scenarios, materials, and a next step after generation; otherwise, the feature stays a novelty.

So my key design question was:

> How can we lower the first step, then create reasons to pay and share?

</section>

I focused on four challenges:

- **Scenario entries**: Put fan support, holidays, relationships, and hot topics upfront so users know why to create.
- **Playful remixes**: Turn old-song memes and trending topics into one-tap adaptations.
- **Ranks and honors**: Use plays, charts, and certificates to make AI songs visible.
- **Business loop**: Connect generation to paid release, promotion, rewards, and retention.

### 1. Scenario-based Creation Entry
![QQ Music AI songwriting user flow](/assets/images/AIMusic/userflow.webp)

I first mapped the **AI songwriting user flow**: choose a scenario, enter content, generate a song, then publish or share.

For example, QQ Music has a large fan community, so I placed **fan support and confession** scenarios upfront. After choosing an artist, users could turn what they wanted to say into a light support song. My work was to break AI songwriting into clear steps: **scenario entry, material guidance, simple creation, and sharing output**.


### 2. Trending Adaptation and Playful Creation

![QQ Music AI song adaptation](/assets/images/AIMusic/Song%20Adaptation.webp)

AI song adaptation was a key playful creation feature. Inspired by old songs being remixed into meme versions on Douyin, I designed a **one-tap adaptation flow**: choose a topic, generate lyrics, then make light choices around voice and style. Users could get something topical, emotional, and shareable without touching complex parameters.

### 3. Ranking and Honor Incentives

![QQ Music AI creation ranking and rewards](/assets/images/AIMusic/Ranking%20List%20%26%20Rewards.webp)

To encourage repeat creation, I proposed **rankings, chart support, and honor certificates**. This moved AI songs from one-off files into a public creative space where works could be seen, compared, and displayed. Users could see performance, gain identity, and feel encouraged to create or share again.

### 4. Commercial Incentive Loop

![QQ Music AI creation business conversion](/assets/images/AIMusic/Business%20Conversion.webp)

For commercialization, I guided users from "trying it once" toward higher-value **creation and operation services**. Paid release, music promotion, and rewards helped AI songs support conversion, creator retention, and content growth. My work was to make these business paths appear naturally when users wanted better results, more exposure, or more return.

## Project Summary

This project clarified a simple point for me: AI songwriting is not about placing generation capability in front of users. The key is giving them a concrete reason to create. QQ Music users may not start with the goal of "making a song," but they do have real motivations such as **fan support, trending expression, relationship communication, and playful participation**.

My design turned those motivations into a lightweight path: **scenario entries** answer "why create," **material guidance and simple creation** lower the barrier, **rankings and honors** provide feedback, and **paid release, promotion, and rewards** connect to higher-value needs. The value was turning one-time AI generation into a playable, shareable music creation ecosystem.
