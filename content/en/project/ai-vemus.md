## What Is Vemus Weiyin?

<p class="vemus-copy">
Vemus Weiyin is an independent AI music creation app extended from <a href="/en/project/qqmusic">QQ Music AI singing and songwriting</a>. We first validated AI song creation and AI singing inside QQ Music, then migrated the core capabilities into Vemus after the direction proved viable.
</p>

<p class="vemus-copy">
The value of an independent app is turning "generating one song" into a fuller creation journey: inspiration input, work generation, AI singing, AI MV, publishing, content consumption, and creator growth.
</p>

![AI song creation mobile interface inside QQ Music](/assets/images/AIMusic/QQMusicAI.webp)

<p class="vemus-intro">
I participated in building Vemus from 0 to 1, leading product and interaction design across the core creation and consumption flows, including the home page, creation, work management, publishing and sharing, content consumption, AI Agent, creator incentives, and recommendation/distribution.
</p>

## Vemus Weiyin: Centering on "Works" to Connect Creative Supply and Content Consumption

<p class="vemus-copy">
The product structure of Vemus is built around "works." One side is creators' content productivity, and the other side is listeners' discovery, listening, and interaction. We connect the original music consumption experience with the work assets generated by AI song creation, AI singing, and AI MV into the same loop.
</p>

<p class="vemus-copy">
This structure allows content supply, consumption activity, creator retention, work dissemination, and paid conversion to continuously flow back into the same system.
</p>

![Vemus system map connecting creators and listeners around music works](/assets/images/Vemus/Vemus_IAmap.png)


## Creation Page

<p class="vemus-copy">
The creation home page organizes conversational song creation, inspiration-based song creation, lyric-based song creation, image-based song creation, and AI singing in one place, allowing users to quickly choose an entry point based on their materials, goals, and current state of inspiration.
</p>

![Vemus AI song creation, AI singing, and AI video creation entry points](/assets/images/Vemus/MainFeatures.png)

## Image-driven AIMV Creation

<p class="vemus-copy">
Image-to-song connects photo upload, style selection, image analysis, and highlight video generation into a complete flow, allowing users to quickly generate music videos with visual storytelling from album materials.
</p>

![Vemus screens for uploading images, choosing music style, analyzing photos, and generating highlight videos](/assets/images/Vemus/Images%20to%20AIMV.png)

## AI Agent

<p class="vemus-copy">
AI Agent serves as a more natural creation entry point. Users can express needs through conversation and progressively complete song creation, listening, adding songs to playlists, and managing conversation history, turning a complex AI creation flow into a continuous conversational experience.
</p>

![Vemus AI Agent conversational song creation](/assets/images/Vemus/AI%20Agent.png)

## Ranking List and Creator Honors

<p class="vemus-copy">
The ranking list is not only a content sorting design. We care more about whether it helps creators feel that their works are being seen. Around rankings, music waves, ranking-share moments, and phased tasks, we tried to transform originally scattered data such as plays, interactions, and fan growth into clearer growth feedback, giving creators a reason to keep operating a work after publishing a song.
</p>

![Vemus ranking list and creator honors interface](/assets/images/Vemus/Honors.png)

## Coins and Growth Incentives

<p class="vemus-copy">
The coin system may initially look like a task system, but the real problem it needs to solve is how to encourage users to keep listening, interacting, creating, and sharing without interrupting the core experience. We mapped different product behaviors to rewards and further connected them with benefit redemption and cash-out mechanisms, hoping the incentive system would serve a real content loop instead of making users act only to complete tasks.
</p>

![Vemus coin tasks and growth incentive interface](/assets/images/Vemus/Coins.png)
