## OMNI ART EXPO

In 2021, during the pandemic, many international students in the United States were unable to complete offline graduation design exhibitions. Together with alumni in China, I initiated and organized an art school exhibition connecting student associations from 10 schools across the United States.

The project collected more than 1,000 submissions and ultimately selected more than 200 works, presenting a public art school exhibition. This project was not only an exhibition organization effort, but also an attempt to give overseas art and design students an offline graduation showcase during a special period.

Together with alumni, we eventually chose Xintai Warehouse, a century-old building in Shanghai, as the exhibition venue. The opening event received support and visits from the Shanghai municipal government, giving this student- and alumni-initiated exhibition broader public attention.
