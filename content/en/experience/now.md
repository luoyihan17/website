I joined Tencent Music Entertainment's QQ Music design team in 2024, mainly responsible for product experience design in AI music creation, AI singing, smart terminals, and innovative music interaction experiences. Since joining, I have been deeply involved in productizing QQ Music's AI creation capabilities, continuously optimizing core experiences such as AI song creation, AI singing, AI arrangement, and AIMV around the goal of "lowering the barrier to music creation so everyday users can also participate in creating."

In AI music creation, I participated in and led the design of features including one-sentence song creation, conversational song creation, humming-to-song, image-to-song, music recipes, and AI generation process visualization. I focused on optimizing input cost, creation paths, waiting experience, and result controllability, helping AI creation move from a "technical capability demo" into a music experience that is easier to understand, more participatory, and more shareable, while supporting business growth in user activity and revenue.

At the same time, I also participated in smart terminal and cross-device projects such as QQ Music TV, HarmonyOS, and Xiaomi Lite. Around listening behavior in different device scenarios, I designed experiences including player upgrades, lock-screen lyrics, status-bar lyrics, transliterated lyrics, healing mode, and karaoke entry points, improving QQ Music's consistency and scenario-based expression across the multi-device ecosystem.

Since 2025, I have further explored Vibe Coding and gamified music interaction. Under limited resources, I independently explored new business possibilities, connected with business teams and developers, "sold" my own game concepts, and successfully launched and deployed them. I also condensed my AI Coding game-making experience into a course and continuously shared my AI experience through Tencent Music Academy.

![Sharing AI game-making experience at Tencent Music Academy](/assets/images/tencent-music/ai-game-course-sharing.jpg)

Overall, my work at QQ Music focuses on translating complex technical capabilities in AI music creation and cross-device music experiences into product experiences users can understand, participate in, and share. I also continue to explore how designers in the AI era can move from "interface designers" toward "experience definers and prototype producers."
