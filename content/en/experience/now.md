I joined Tencent Music Entertainment's QQ Music design team in 2024. I work on AI music creation, AI singing, connected-device experiences, and new forms of music interaction. My main focus has been turning AI creation capabilities into products around one goal: lowering the barrier to music creation so everyday users can participate.

In AI music creation, I led or contributed to songwriting from a single sentence, conversational songwriting, humming-to-song, image-to-song, music recipes, and generation visualization. I focused on input effort, creation paths, waiting states, and result control, turning AI from a capability demo into a music experience users can understand, participate in, and share.

I also worked on smart-device and cross-device projects such as QQ Music TV, HarmonyOS, and Xiaomi Lite. Across different listening contexts, I designed player upgrades, lock-screen lyrics, status-bar lyrics, transliterated lyrics, healing mode, and karaoke entry points, improving consistency across devices.

Since 2025, I have been exploring vibe coding and gamified music interaction. With limited resources, I prototyped new business possibilities, connected business and development teams, pitched playable music experiences, and shipped them. I also turned my AI coding game work into a course for Tencent Music Academy.

![Sharing AI game-making experience at Tencent Music Academy](/assets/images/tencent-music/ai-game-course-sharing.webp)

Overall, my work at QQ Music turns complex AI and cross-device capabilities into experiences users can understand, participate in, and share. It has also expanded my role as a designer: from making interfaces to defining experiences and producing working prototypes.
