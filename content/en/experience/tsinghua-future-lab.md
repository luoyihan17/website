In 2021, I worked as an interaction design and research intern at Tsinghua Future Laboratory. I joined interdisciplinary projects across smart mobility, virtual consumption, senior-friendly design, and physiological data interaction. My work covered interface prototypes, user research, experiment execution, and academic research. I helped design a campus autonomous vehicle management system that supported real-time testing and supervision for seven vehicles, and contributed to interaction models for traffic management. I also worked on an Alibaba virtual mall project, supporting interaction flow, interface, and motion design for consumer experiences in virtual space.

Beyond product and system design, I researched how older adults use smart products, using interviews, observation, and synthesis to identify barriers across age groups. I also joined EEG research around sleep activity, exploring how physiological data could become perceivable interactive art. During the internship, I assisted with two academic papers and research materials, and built a complete workflow from problem discovery to user research, interaction design, experiment validation, and research summary. It was my first time deeply combining design practice with academic research, and it shaped my later exploration of HCI, immersive interaction, and emerging technology experiences.
