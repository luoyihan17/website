I worked as a VR UX Designer at USC World Building Media Lab, leading interaction flow and UI design for the World In A Cell VR educational game.

Main work included:

- Improved immersion and learning efficiency through user-centered interaction design;
- Led 30+ usability and scenario tests, then turned findings into optimizations;
- Collaborated with USC biological scientists on a VR cell biology tool;
- Helped introduce the project into USC biology courses in Fall 2023.
