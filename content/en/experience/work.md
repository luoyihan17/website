I worked as a VR UX Designer at USC World Building Media Lab, where I led the interaction flow and interface design for **World in a Cell**, a VR educational game for cell biology.

My work included:

- Improving immersion and learning efficiency through user-centered interaction design;
- Leading 30+ usability and scenario-based tests, then translating findings into design improvements;
- Collaborating with USC biology researchers on a VR tool for cell biology education;
- Helping bring the project into USC biology courses in Fall 2023.
