## International Robot Art Exhibition

As an accompanying art and design exhibition for the 2025 World Robot Conference, this International Robotics and Art Design Biennale took "Future Symbionts: Robots, Art, and Humanity" as its theme, exploring how robotics can gradually shift from industrial production tools into media for culture, emotion, and artistic expression.

I participated in the full process from curatorial positioning to exhibition delivery, including curatorial theme definition, exhibition structure planning, international open calls, work review, spatial content organization, visitor flow design, and coordination with partner institutions. The project invited submissions from leading domestic art schools, overseas art and design colleges, young artists, robot designers, and interdisciplinary research teams. It received more than 200 works globally and ultimately selected more than 40 for exhibition.

The exhibition covered robot product design, AI art, intelligent interactive installations, bionic machinery, 3D printing, future home design, and conceptual design. By combining an indoor technology art exhibition with outdoor scenario-based experiences, it translated complex robotics technology into a cultural experience the public could view, understand, and participate in.

This project shifted me from being the designer of a single work to the builder of an interdisciplinary content system. I not only had to judge the value of each work, but also define the relationships among works, coordinate artists, schools, industry institutions, space, and public experience, and ultimately build a platform for exchange among robotics, art, design, and society.
