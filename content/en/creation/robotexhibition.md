## International Robot Art Exhibition

As an accompanying exhibition for the 2025 World Robot Conference, this biennale used the theme "Future Symbionts: Robots, Art, and Humanity" to explore how robotics can move from industrial tools into media for culture, emotion, and artistic expression.

I participated from curatorial positioning to delivery, including theme definition, exhibition structure, international open calls, work review, spatial content, visitor flow, and partner coordination. The project received 200+ global works and selected 40+ for exhibition.

The exhibition covered robot product design, AI art, interactive installations, bionic machinery, 3D printing, future home design, and conceptual design. It translated complex robotics into a cultural experience the public could view, understand, and join.

This project shifted me from designing a single work to building an interdisciplinary content system across artists, schools, industry partners, space, and public experience.
